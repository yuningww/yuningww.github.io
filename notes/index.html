These notes are my own summary on various topics including information theory, coding theory, and probabilistic machine learning. The goal is to make the concepts, algorithms, derivations more concise than the textbooks, and easy to look up. Personally, I use these notes for me to look up core concepts and equations during my study, research, and daily work, which I found quite useful. As I continue to read and learn more in these fields, I will continue to update these notes to make them more complete, rather than focusing on several topics only.
